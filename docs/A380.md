# A380-800

Flight Training includes three A380 exercises (14–16) on the coastal training
runway: four-engine takeoff (450 t), configured approach/landing (380 t), and
outboard engine 1 failure after takeoff (430 t). These exercises have independent
progress IDs within the unified 16-exercise training list. Training cards label
the aircraft model. The original A320 lessons, airport flights and US1549 retain
their existing aircraft and progress.

## Airframe and reference data

The authored exterior uses Airbus's published 72.7 m length, 79.8 m span,
24.1 m overall height, 845 m² reference wing area and nominal 70,000 lbf
(approximately 311.4 kN) per-engine thrust. See the
[Airbus facts and figures](https://www.airbus.com/sites/default/files/2021-12/EN-Airbus-A380-Facts-and-Figures-December-2021_0.pdf).
The full-length double-deck body, four hollow nacelles/fans, wingtip fences,
two wing gear, two body gear and twin-wheel nose gear are original authored
geometry. There are 22 rotating wheels. Detailed shapes, mount locations and
contact envelopes are visual/game estimates rather than manufacturer CAD.

Only engines 2 and 3 have reverse capability, matching the inboard-only installation
described in the [Airbus airport planning manual, section 2-12-0](https://www.airbus.com/sites/default/files/2021-11/Airbus-Aircraft-AC-A380.pdf).
Engines 1 and 4 spool toward idle during reverse and never generate reverse
thrust or animate reversing cowls. A failed inboard engine cannot reverse.

## Handling and systems

`game/src/aircraft/definitions/a380.json` owns mass, coefficients, engine moment
arms, control/actuator rates, contacts and visual bindings. The common rate-servo
adapter has slower pitch/roll response and the engines spool more slowly than
the A320. Four engine states and audio voices remain independent; the A380
voices use a lower frequency and a four-voice gain preset. Rudder assistance
accounts for asymmetric thrust. Translational motion remains force-integrated.

The cockpit uses the established simplified photographic layout, shared FCU
controls and four independent N1 gauges with five gear indications. It does not
reproduce a complete A380 flight deck or its certified control laws. The training
radio retains the existing flight callsign, which is independent of aircraft type.

Rotation is prompted at 150 kt, to approximately 10°. In the landing exercise,
CONF FULL targets 150 kt. These and all flap limits, thrust lag, aero coefficients,
control gains, failure triggers and scoring thresholds are calibrated game
presets, not real-world flight planning or operating limits. The landing entry
is farther out to permit localizer capture from below the glide path. The player
must configure, pass both stability gates, fly the manual final and stop.

## Model and paint contract

- Template: `ops/aircraft/a380.py`, using `ops/aircraft_geometry.py`.
- UV contract: `ops/assets/aircraft/a380-uv1.json`.
- Runtime model: `public/assets/aircraft/skyline-380-uv1.glb`.
- Original YUNJI paint: `ops/build-a380-livery.py`, independent `a380-uv1` atlas.
- Catalog: `game/src/aircraft/a380-livery-manifest.json`, mirrored by
  `public/assets/aircraft/liveries/a380-manifest.json`.

The unbatched exterior has 59,338 triangles and 9 materials. Runtime batching
retains every actuator and wheel axis. Four fans and 22 independently rotating
wheels add draw submissions relative to the A320; only the selected model/paint
is retained. The same 2K/4K texture selection and local KTX2 decoder apply. A 2K
BC7 mip chain uses approximately 5.33 MiB; the 4K chain approximately 21.33 MiB.
Switching aircraft waits for the selected model and paint before enabling play.

```sh
python3 ops/build-a380-livery.py --toktx /path/to/toktx
blender -b --python ops/build-aircraft.py -- --model a380 \
  --output-dir /tmp/a380-review --skip-preview
node ops/check-aircraft-liveries.mjs
node --test game/tests/a380.test.mjs game/tests/aircraft-foundation.test.mjs
```

Omit `--skip-preview` for a native Blender render. Normal checkout builds use
the checked-in GLB and KTX2 files, without requiring Blender, CairoSVG or toktx.
Keep the definition/asset/UV/paint versions immutable after release. Checkpoints,
retries and replay retain the A380 identity and all four sampled engines. Older
recordings continue to resolve to A320 version 1.
