from Phosphor Icons.

Rebuild the registered cockpit background with
`node ops/build-cockpit-fcu-frame.mjs`. Its source crop is recorded in
`ops/assets/cockpit-fcu-recess.json`. The live FCU has explicit +/- keys and
mode buttons inside this background aperture; no decorative rotary knobs or
separate raised faceplate are rendered. Autoflight mode keys with green state
indicators are mounted inside the aperture. The former decorative amber lamp
is replaced with adjacent original panel texture by the SVG composition.
The SVG and live controls share `game/src/views/fcu-layout.json` coordinates.
The transparent virtual stick remains independent in the lower-left gutter.
`node ops/check-fcu-fit.mjs` checks the actual components against the measured
black interior in the source artwork at multiple viewport sizes and languages,
including +/- actions, mode indicators and the vertical stick arrangement.

## Speech

Whole-line speech sources are self-hosted as mono MP3 at 24 kHz / 48 kbps:

- `public/assets/radio/` and `en/` retain base/legacy dialogue and the shared
  English height/safety callouts. Historical files remain available to open sessions.
- `public/assets/radio/pilots/` supplies the five Chinese cast members in both
  roles, plus separate unfiltered pilot recordings.
- `public/assets/radio/en/pilots/` supplies unfiltered English Guy recordings.

The airport, airline and ground dialogue extensions are described below. These are
synthetic recordings of fictional exchanges. Callsigns, training frequency,
clearances and emergencies are game scenarios.

| Role | Chinese dialogue | English dialogue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tower | E / G / H / J / Xiaoxiao, chosen per airport | `en-US-AriaNeural`, +22%, −6 Hz |
| Pilot | Same five-speaker pool; different from the tower | `en-US-GuyNeural`, +24%, −10 Hz |
| Shared safety/height calls | `en-US-GuyNeural`, +6%, 0 Hz | Same files |

Chinese radio uses Fish Audio E/G/H/J and Edge Xiaoxiao (+24%, −12 Hz).
English and legacy clips use edge-tts. Both pipelines normalize with FFmpeg; legacy radio sources also have baked band limiting. New pilot dry sources preserve the full speech band.
They are outputs, not voice models. Provider software, voice-model rights and
service terms are not conveyed by this repository's MIT license. See
[third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

Text, measured durations and cache fingerprints are in `game/src/comms/`.
The optional `ops/generate-radio.py` tool can regenerate recordings. Initial
MAYDAY/PAN PAN declarations contain three complete units from the same pilot
voice as the following report; `ops/verify-radio-prefixes.py` checks waveforms.

Runtime playback is entirely self-hosted. Web Audio adds radio band limiting,
compression, distortion, gated static and PTT clicks; clean safety calls have
priority. Engine, wind, rolling, reverse-volume and contact effects are
procedurally synthesized in `game/src/audio/soundscape.js`.

Routine gameplay radio uses shared phrase recipes in
`game/src/comms/phrase-catalogue.json`, authored from the existing approved
performances and the accepted audition takes. Phrase WAVs and independent donor
recordings are under `public/assets/radio/phrases/`. Runtime PCM assembly keeps
the original actor and speech rate, then applies the existing live effects. Old
whole takes remain available as complete fallbacks. New recipes can generate a
whole fallback from shared phrases without another recording. See
[Radio phrases](docs/RADIO-PHRASES.md) for authoring, validation and cache limits.

footprint. Bronze roof shape, height, glazing, jet bridges, parked aircraft,
support buildings and runway-area grading are simplified visual estimates.
Architectural photographs and commercial airport models are not bundled.

approach, missed-approach return and final. They use the same voice profiles
and normalization as the training radio; measured durations and a separate
cache fingerprint are in `game/src/comms/daxing-radio.json`. They are fictional
game exchanges, not recordings or operational instructions from Daxing ATC.

Twelve Chinese and twelve English `ground-*.mp3` clips cover push/start approval,

tiles extend across the airport and22km arrival corridor, and one covers

Original source-aligned SoFi/Forum/skyline recognition geometry is documented

The continuous city pack covers available source footprints across the full
60 km LAX terrain: 1,894,606 source buildings in 947 source chunks and 263
shared far draw batches. `buildings-v2.bin.gz` is 160.4 MiB and loads only at
LAX; its manifest records
exact count/bytes, source/output hashes, ODbL attribution and height provenance.
Repeated homes share original flat/hip/gable templates, with compact 39-byte
instance parameters and shared vertex arrays. Near complex outlines/courtyards
retain source geometry; middle-distance ordinary buildings share boxes; distant
low buildings use source-derived district massing while towers stay individual.

heights/colours are visual estimates, not surveyed reconstructions.
Roof colours sample NAIP, nearby roofs share the terrain's high-resolution
image cache, and facade colours vary by stable neighbourhood/source-ID seeds.

128 KiB procedural map. The original airport/terminal models remain intact.

[Data/rebuild notes](game/src/airports/data/LAX-CITY.md).

### Failure-scene radio montage

`game/src/failure/script.json` contains original bilingual field reports and
instructor lines. Emergency-role casting is explicit in
`game/src/failure/cast.json`: Chinese field reports/instructor use Xiaoxiao,
dispatch uses Yunxi and responding units use Yunjian. English uses Andrew for
field reports/instructor, Guy for dispatch and Aria for responding units.
Yunxi and Yunjian are additional Edge voices for this emergency scene; the
normal crew and airport controller casts retain their established identities.
Controller calls and short
acknowledgements are curated excerpts of the existing approved recordings;
`game/src/failure/voice-assets.json` records source paths, hashes and excerpt
ranges. The flight's selected controller is retained. These are game
performances, not historical accident or police recordings.

The active manifest references 223 content-addressed files in
`public/assets/radio/failure/`, including 148 pre-rendered mixes (about 20 MiB
for the active pack). Earlier
published versions remain available to open sessions. Each mix retains
normal speech speed while combining distinct voices, narrow-band distortion,
carrier dropouts, synthetic noise and distant siren/boat ambience. The final
confirmation has less masking as the background continuously fades. Field
reports and final confirmations have their own narrow-band, saturated radio
channel with carrier noise and opening/closing squelch, in addition to the scene
ambience. Dispatch includes brief noisy dropouts while a slightly wider radio
band preserves the distinction between the three principal speakers. Runtime
streams a single mix and uses its measured cue timeline for captions.

To regenerate, install `ops/requirements-tts.txt`, run
`ops/generate-failure-audio.py`, then `ops/build-failure-mixes.py` and
`node ops/check-failure-audio.mjs`. Upgrade the mix profile identity when
changing DSP. Ordinary checkout tests/builds use the bundled media and do not

### Fixed historical crew and successful water rescue

US1549 captain and first-officer casting is fixed in
`game/src/history/crew-cast.json`: Chinese H / G and English Guy / Andrew.
The established synthetic actors were selected for a restrained, lower captain
and a distinct, brighter procedural first officer, with public interviews as
casting references ([Sullenberger, 2009](https://abcnews.com/video/47427104/),
[Skiles, 2009](https://charlierose.com/videos/14176)). This is an artistic
casting direction, not a measured voice match or a voice clone. These existing
actors remain available to ordinary missions; historical retries and language
changes keep the same role mapping. Controllers remain separate from both crew.

`game/src/history/rescue-script.json` and `rescue-assets.json` define original
successful-ditching field reports, dispatch and responding units. They reuse
the emergency cast in `game/src/failure/cast.json`, the selected controller's
existing rescue call and the fixed first officer's existing closing line.
Eight 19-second mixes cover both languages, eligible controllers and controlled /
damaged survival. The active source/mix set is about 1.5 MiB. Synthetic distant
shore sirens, water wash and approaching boat engines fade under the dry closing
line. Other transmissions have individual radio band-limiting, distortion,
carrier noise and squelch; dispatch contains a short interference burst.
The scene confirms flotation and evacuation in progress, without inventing an
all-passengers-rescued outcome. Rebuild with `ops/generate-rescue-audio.py` using
the existing TTS requirements, then run `node ops/check-rescue-audio.mjs`.

### Historical opening dialogue and bird-ingestion effects

`history/opening-script.json` / `opening-assets.json` contain six normal
departure calls for the fixed captain and eligible controllers. Chinese calls
join approved same-actor performances: the existing Cactus 1549 prefix and the
routine planned-route dialogue, omitting the unrelated fictional callsign.
The manifest preserves every source fingerprint and measured excerpt range.
English retains the existing Guy/Aria profiles. All clips play at normal speed;
no real accident audio or new cloned voices are used. Regenerate with
`ops/generate-history-opening.py`, then validate with
`node ops/check-history-opening.mjs`.

`audio/bird-strike.js` synthesizes original low-frequency, closely spaced dull
strikes followed by two decelerating engine-shaft layers and fading airflow.
Its bounded stereo buffer is prepared during normal climbing, resumes at the
current flight-time offset after pause and remains continuous across the cockpit
cut. It does not replace the established engine bed or change engine physics.
`history/engine-smoke.js` adds one bounded, camera-facing instanced puff system
at the shared authored nacelle anchors. The gray ingestion trails fade over time,
use no bitmap assets, and never enter collision geometry or recorded state.
