The cockpit frame is pre-rendered; instruments and controls are live. Exterior
terrain, runway markings, lights, buildings, static aircraft and vegetation
placement are generated by Three.js code. The training airport is fictional;
the Daxing airport scene uses the geographic data described below. UI icons come
from Phosphor Icons.

Rebuild the registered cockpit background with
`node ops/build-cockpit-fcu-frame.mjs`. Its source crop is recorded in
`ops/assets/cockpit-fcu-recess.json`. The live FCU has explicit +/- keys and
mode buttons inside this background aperture; no decorative rotary knobs or
separate raised faceplate are rendered. Autoflight mode keys with green state
indicators are mounted inside the aperture. The former decorative amber lamp
is replaced with adjacent original panel texture by the SVG composition.
The SVG and live controls share `game/src/views/fcu-layout.json` coordinates.
The transparent virtual stick remains independent in the lower-left gutter.
`node ops/check-fcu-fit.mjs` checks the actual components against the measured
black interior in the source artwork at multiple viewport sizes and languages,
including +/- actions, mode indicators and the vertical stick arrangement.

## Speech

The game includes **1,435 mono MP3 files** at 24 kHz / 48 kbps:

- `public/assets/radio/`: 202 base/legacy Chinese dialogue clips and 21 shared English
  height/safety callouts.
- `public/assets/radio/en/`: 202 English pilot/tower dialogue clips.
- `public/assets/radio/pilots/`: 1,010 Chinese pilot/controller clips across
  the five selected speakers.

The airport, airline and ground dialogue extensions are described below. These are
synthetic recordings of fictional exchanges. Callsigns, training frequency,
clearances and emergencies are game scenarios.

| Role | Chinese dialogue | English dialogue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tower | E / G / H / J / Xiaoxiao, chosen once per flight | `en-US-AriaNeural`, +22%, −6 Hz |
| Pilot | Same five-speaker pool; different from the tower | `en-US-GuyNeural`, +24%, −10 Hz |
| Shared safety/height calls | `en-US-GuyNeural`, +6%, 0 Hz | Same files |

Chinese radio uses Fish Audio E/G/H/J and Edge Xiaoxiao (+24%, −12 Hz).
English and legacy clips use edge-tts. Both pipelines filter/normalize using FFmpeg.
They are outputs, not voice models. Provider software, voice-model rights and
service terms are not conveyed by this repository's MIT license. See
[third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

Text, measured durations and cache fingerprints are in `game/src/comms/`.
The optional `ops/generate-radio.py` tool can regenerate recordings. Initial
MAYDAY/PAN PAN declarations contain three complete units from the same pilot
voice as the following report; `ops/verify-radio-prefixes.py` checks waveforms.

Runtime playback is entirely self-hosted. Web Audio adds radio band limiting,
compression, distortion, gated static and PTT clicks; clean safety calls have
priority. Engine, wind, rolling, reverse-volume and contact effects are
procedurally synthesized in `game/src/audio/soundscape.js`.

## Daxing airport scene

`game/src/airports/data/daxing.json` contains real OSM geographic geometry,
OurAirports runway endpoints and Mapzen/USGS terrain heights. Data sources and
their separate licenses are documented in [the pack notes](game/src/airports/data/README.md)
and [third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). The build supplies the same
database as `assets/maps/daxing-source.json` for attribution and data sharing.
No public OSM raster tiles are scraped or included.

The continuous city layer additionally combines the pinned 2026-11-20 Hebei /
Beijing OSM extract with Shi et al. (2023) CC BY 4.0 East Asian building outlines
for Beijing, Langfang and Baoding. Self-hosted Copernicus Sentinel-2 imagery from
2025-10-13 supplies native 10 m ground pixels and footprint-weighted roof colours.
Northern village/estate/logistics heights and neutral facades are estimates when
source tags are missing. Shared templates, advance preparation and IndexedDB
reuse follow the other city worlds. [Sources and rebuilding](game/src/airports/data/DAXING-CITY.md).

The Daxing terminal is an original procedural mesh built from its OSM outer
footprint. Bronze roof shape, height, glazing, jet bridges, parked aircraft,
support buildings and runway-area grading are simplified visual estimates.
Architectural photographs and commercial airport models are not bundled.
The roof palette follows the user's selected
[CCTV aerial photograph](https://p1.img.cctvpic.com/cportal/img/2019/9/16/1568613858629_341_1000x1174.jpg)
and [China Daily photograph](https://img3.chinadaily.com.cn/images/201909/25/5d8afdfba31099ab43d18ba3.jpeg):
orange-copper metal, golden highlights, red-brown shade and dark blue-grey
glazing. These photographs are references only and are not distributed assets.

Eight Chinese and eight English `daxing-*.mp3` clips cover runway 17L takeoff,
approach, missed-approach return and final. They use the same voice profiles
and normalization as the training radio; measured durations and a separate
cache fingerprint are in `game/src/comms/daxing-radio.json`. They are fictional
game exchanges, not recordings or operational instructions from Daxing ATC.

Twelve Chinese and twelve English `ground-*.mp3` clips cover push/start approval,
taxi clearance, holding, runway entry, taxi-speed reminders and hold-short
corrections. They retain the existing pilot/tower voice profiles and radio DSP.
`game/src/comms/ground-radio.json` supplies scripts, per-language durations and
the cache fingerprint. Ground uses the tower voice to avoid adding another
character. The crew handles all radio actions automatically.

The derived departure route is in `game/src/airports/data/daxing-departure.json`,
with source feature IDs and explicitly labelled game holding/lineup positions.
It is also downloadable as `assets/maps/daxing-departure.json` (ODbL).

## LAX city imagery and landmarks

`public/assets/cities/lax/` contains nine genuine USDA NAIP RGB orthophoto
WebPs captured11 May2022 (overview4000×2650 plus eight1024² details), totalling
6,242,846 bytes. These are public-domain source imagery with attribution, not
generated photographs. `imagery.json` records all31 source COGs, acquisition
dates, source/output hashes, bounds and reprojection/resampling. Original COG
reads are build-time only; no imagery provider is contacted during gameplay.

`sea-mask.png` is a16,020-byte north-up grayscale derivative of the existing
LAX OSM ocean polygon, including its island holes. It masks coarse submerged
terrain triangles without changing the land DEM. The original nine colour images use about
101MB of GPU memory including mipmaps; the single-channel mask adds10.6MB.

The current LAX loader uses the separately fingerprinted `imagery-lod.json`:
a2048² outer-region base, the original city overview, nineteen2048² detail
WebPs and two source-derived sea masks. Its114 NAIP catalog records span
24 April–13 May2022, with exact per-source dates retained. Eighteen detail
tiles extend across the airport and22km arrival corridor, and one covers
Downtown. Only three detail slots are sampled, with a four-texture cache;
estimated imagery/mipmap/mask residency stays below200MiB. Original images
and their manifest remain available for reproducibility. Build with
`ops/build-lax-imagery-lod.py`; gameplay uses no external imagery service.

Original source-aligned SoFi/Forum/skyline recognition geometry is documented
in [landmark notes](game/src/airports/lax-city-landmarks-notes.md). The16
landmarks use16,031 triangles before shared-material batching, including
metre-scaled tower glazing and recessed floor belts.

The continuous city pack covers available source footprints across the full
60 km LAX terrain: 1,894,606 source buildings in 947 source chunks and 263
shared far draw batches. `buildings-v2.bin.gz` is 160.4 MiB and loads only at
LAX; its manifest records
exact count/bytes, source/output hashes, ODbL attribution and height provenance.
Repeated homes share original flat/hip/gable templates, with compact 39-byte
instance parameters and shared vertex arrays. Near complex outlines/courtyards
retain source geometry; middle-distance ordinary buildings share boxes; distant
low buildings use source-derived district massing while towers stay individual.
All middle/far chunks prepare before flight, nearby detail warms in every
direction, and all prepared variants remain cached until world disposal.
GPU buffers are managed independently. Full source contact geometry remains
resident and separate from render LOD. Template architecture and inferred
heights/colours are visual estimates, not surveyed reconstructions.
Roof colours sample NAIP, nearby roofs share the terrain's high-resolution
image cache, and facade colours vary by stable neighbourhood/source-ID seeds.
No additional bitmap facade textures are created; tower glazing retains its
128 KiB procedural map. The original airport/terminal models remain intact.
The former v1 pack is retained for reproducibility/cached earlier previews.

The old1,507-building sample remains as a legacy source/physics regression
fixture and is not rendered together with the continuous pack. Geographic
derivatives retain ODbL; original generation/rendering code remains MIT.
[Data/rebuild notes](game/src/airports/data/LAX-CITY.md).

The LAX terminal realism pass is original code-authored geometry with five
procedural DataTextures (448KiB raw before mipmaps). It adds curtain walls,
panel joints, roof seams and services, skylights, structural piers, canopies
and detailed jet bridges. No reference photo, purchased mesh or third-party
terminal texture is bundled. [Terminal notes](game/src/airports/lax-scene-notes.md).

### Chinese pilot voice auditions

`public/assets/pilot-voices/` is an isolated listening page with four alternatives
(Yunjian, YunJhe, Yunxia and Xiaoyi) and the former Yunxi reference. The 30 neural
